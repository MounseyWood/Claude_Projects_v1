# The Thread and the Loop

An interactive, phone-first vertical piece for FN4005 undergraduates about polyester and recycling. Sixteen full-screen frames snap like a social feed. One Verlet-simulated thread runs through all of them and is the controller: you pull it, drag it, fork it, melt it, stretch it and knot it, and it keeps what you do. The care label at the end reads the thread back and decides whether the loop can close. Text blocks are solid: the thread routes round them.

Frames: the thread lassos the title · links rise out of an oil field onto the thread · 1941 written by the thread · pull the thread down to draw the fibre (a field of molecular chains lines up) · drag across the grid to guess, then the thread moves to 59% · tug-of-war, the thread is the rope · the washing machine is the interface and the water rises with every fibre shed · bottle → shirt → stops (the frame changes colour each step) · tap the coil to add elastane, tap the ring to recycle (it jams) · the thread forks: pick the PET, the other strand falls away · pull the spring, drag the elastane off · two plies, scrub the temperature, melted polymer floods the screen · the lab rubber is spliced into your thread · each promise ties a knot · care label and the open or closed loop · notes and sources.
