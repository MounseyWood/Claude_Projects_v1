# The Thread and the Loop

An interactive, phone-first vertical piece for FN4005 undergraduates about polyester and recycling. Sixteen full-screen frames snap like a social feed. One Verlet-simulated thread runs through all of them, bends into each idea, and **keeps what you do to it**: how far it was drawn, the fraying from the wash, any elastane wrapped round it, any PLA spliced in. The last frame reads the thread back and decides whether the loop can close. Text blocks are solid: the thread routes round them, and they wobble when it hits them.

Frames: what PET is (tap to add links) · 1941 · melt spinning (draw ratio changes the thread for the rest of the scroll) · guess the share of fibre (the thread follows your guess, then moves to 59%) · weigh it up (a balance scale made of the thread) · microfibres (wash or rub; the thread stays frayed) · bottle → shirt → stops · add elastane, then recycle (it jams, and the elastane stays on your thread) · spot the PET (a wrong pick splices PLA into your thread) · stretch without elastane (pull the spring; swap the elastane out) · heat test (a rising heat block, to scale) · lab-made all-polyester rubber (pull to 11×) · EU rules and designer pledges (swing tags) · your thread's passport and the open or closed loop · notes and sources.
